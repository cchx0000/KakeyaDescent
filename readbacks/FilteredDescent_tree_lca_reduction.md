**Read-back for `FilteredDescent.tree_lca_reduction`.** Fix a type $\alpha$ with decidable equality. Let $T$ be a finite set of finite words (lists) over $\alpha$ satisfying two structural requirements: ($\mathit{hroot}$) the empty word $[]$ belongs to $T$, and ($\mathit{hprefix}$) $T$ is prefix-closed — every prefix $p$ of every word $l \in T$ is itself a member of $T$. Let $u$ assign a real number to each word, with ($\mathit{hu}$) $u_\gamma \ge 0$ for every leaf $\gamma$ of $T$, where the leaves ($\mathrm{treeLeaves}\,T$) are the words $l \in T$ that are maximal under the prefix order (no word of $T$ strictly extends them). Let $B$ be a real number with ($\mathit{hB}$) $B \ge 0$, and let $D$ be a natural number. Assume further: ($\mathit{hproper}$) for every node $a \in T$ other than the empty word, the node aggregate $\mathrm{nodeAgg}\,T\,u\,a$ — defined as the sum of $u_\gamma$ over all leaves $\gamma$ of $T$ of which $a$ is a prefix — is at most $B$; and ($\mathit{hheight}$) every node $a \in T$ has length at most $D$. Then the following inequality holds:

$$\sum_{\gamma \in \mathrm{Leaves}(T)} \sum_{\gamma' \in \mathrm{Leaves}(T)} u_\gamma u_{\gamma'} \;\le\; D \cdot B \cdot \sum_{\gamma \in \mathrm{Leaves}(T)} u_\gamma \;+\; X^{\mathrm{root}}(T, u),$$

where $X^{\mathrm{root}}(T, u)$ ($\mathrm{Xroot}\,T\,u$) is the sum of $u_\gamma u_{\gamma'}$ restricted to those pairs of leaves $(\gamma, \gamma')$ whose least common ancestor is the empty word. The least common ancestor ($\mathrm{treeLCA}$) of two words $\gamma, \gamma'$ is computed as follows: take the largest $k$ in $\{0, 1, \dots, \mathrm{length}(\gamma)\}$ such that the length-$k$ prefix of $\gamma$ is also a prefix of $\gamma'$, and return that prefix (such a $k$ always exists since $k = 0$ works, the empty word being a prefix of everything); this is the longest common prefix of the two words.

The left-hand side is the double sum over all ordered pairs of leaves, which factors as the square of the total leaf mass $(\sum_\gamma u_\gamma)^2$ since every summand $u_\gamma u_{\gamma'} \ge 0$. The right-hand side is $D \cdot B$ times the total leaf mass, plus the root-cross pair mass $X^{\mathrm{root}}$.

Edge cases and immediate consequences. The hypothesis $\mathit{hroot}$ forces $T$ to always contain $[]$, so the statement never applies to an empty tree. If $D = 0$, then $\mathit{hheight}$ forces every node to have length $0$, so $T = \{[]\}$; the bound then reads $u_{[]}^2 \le u_{[]}^2$ because $X^{\mathrm{root}}$ coincides with the full double sum in that case. If $B = 0$, then $\mathit{hproper}$ forces every proper node's descendant-leaf mass to be $\le 0$, hence (with $\mathit{hu}$) exactly $0$, and the conclusion asserts the full leaf-pair sum is $\le X^{\mathrm{root}}$ — but $X^{\mathrm{root}}$ is a sub-sum of it with nonnegative terms, so this forces equality. Note $\mathit{hu}$ constrains $u$ only on leaves; $u$ may take arbitrary (even negative) values on non-leaf words, which never enter the statement. Finally, since a leaf $\gamma \ne []$ has itself as its only leaf descendant, $\mathit{hproper}$ applied to such a leaf says each individual leaf load satisfies $u_\gamma \le B$.
