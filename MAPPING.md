## Known gaps

1. **Trivial history tree.** The descent runs on `trivialTree` (root `[]` with one leaf `[t]` per tube), not the paper's geometric multi-scale history tree. Consequently `Xdup = 0` and the genuine duplicate closure (143)–(155) is never exercised; the geometric content of how tree structure reflects tube interactions is unformalized.
2. **Abstract `ShadedTubes` — now grounded, not yet wired.** `Def_FilteredDescent_PhysicalTubes.lean` (Stream B, this branch) defines real δ-tubes in `EuclideanSpace`, direction separation, measurable shadings, Lebesgue volumes, and proves `physicalRealization : ShadedTubes n` (`mult_eq`, `shade_le_union`, nonnegativity derived from real geometry). The main chain still consumes the abstract `ShadedTubes` interface; swapping in the physical instance is future work.
3. **Standalone modules.** Insertion kernels, Cauchy–Binet, pivot dichotomy, support recurrence, duplicate closure, LCA reduction, and the unweighted-projection counterexample are proved but not wired into the main chain. R5 is now genuinely consumed by `terminal_hardening` (§10.3), which is itself standalone (interface fixed for a parallel stream).
4. **Typed route excluded.** The Hall-capacity comparison (160)/(207) is not formalized and is explicitly excluded from the scalar closure; it carries the independent Hall/Radon–Nikodym positivity requirement the paper does not claim.
5. **Deep estimates are hypotheses.** [2] (planar Córdoba), [7,10] (sticky + sticky/non-sticky), [9] (marked 4D) enter only as named assumptions; only their *consumption* by the descent is proved. d ≥ 5 is unclaimed.
